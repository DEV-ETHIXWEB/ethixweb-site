# Ethixweb off-site SEO playbook

The website side is done: fast pages, keyword titles, structured data,
9 city pages, 9 guides, sitemap, IndexNow and llms.txt. What decides the
Map Pack and most of the remaining ranking is off the site: your Google
Business Profile, Google reviews and links from other sites. Everything
below is ready to copy and paste. Work through it in order.

---

## 0. Decide your public details first (5 minutes)

Every listing must use exactly the same details. Pick them once:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Business name | **Ethixweb** (no keywords added to the name, which breaks Google's rules) |
| Website | https://www.ethixweb.com |
| Email | info@ethixweb.com |
| Phone | **A US local number is needed.** A 206 (Seattle) or 253 (south King / Pierce) number is ideal. Use the same one everywhere. |
| Address | Service-area business: Google needs a real address to verify, but you hide it. Never use a virtual office or PO box. |
| Hours | Mon-Fri 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM Pacific |
| Service areas | Seattle, SeaTac, Tukwila, Kent, Renton, Bellevue, Tacoma, Federal Way, Auburn |

After you choose the phone number, tell Claude: it should go into the site
footer, the contact page and the structured data so they all match.

## 1. Google Search Console (10 minutes, do today)

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console and add a **Domain** property for `ethixweb.com`.
2. Verify with the DNS TXT record (at your domain host, GoDaddy).
3. Sitemaps, then submit: `https://www.ethixweb.com/sitemap_index.xml`
4. URL inspection: request indexing for these, one by one:
   - https://www.ethixweb.com/
   - https://www.ethixweb.com/locations/seattle-wa
   - https://www.ethixweb.com/locations/seatac-wa
   - https://www.ethixweb.com/hvac-plumbing-marketing
   - https://www.ethixweb.com/blog/google-map-pack-for-contractors
5. Also add the site to **Bing Webmaster Tools** (https://www.bing.com/webmasters). You can import it straight from Search Console. Bing powers ChatGPT search and Copilot.

anything in return, and reply to every review within two days.

## 5. Backlinks (ongoing)

The best links for Ethixweb come from client websites and the home
service industry.

**Client footer credit.** Ask each client to add a small footer line
"Website by Ethixweb" linking to https://www.ethixweb.com. Message:

> Hi [Name], small request: would you be OK with a small "Website by Ethixweb" credit in your site footer? It helps other contractors find us. I can add it for you, it's tiny and won't change the design.

**Case study swaps.** Offer clients a short write-up of their results they
can share. Many will link to the case study on their own site or social.

**Industry and local.** Guest articles or quotes for HVAC/plumbing trade
blogs, the chambers above, local business podcasts, and supplier or
software partner directories (ServiceTitan, Jobber and Housecall Pro all
have partner or marketplace listings worth applying to).

Avoid paid link packages and link exchanges. They can get a site penalized.

## How to tell it's working

- **Search Console:** impressions and clicks rising, with the city pages and guides appearing.
- **Business Profile:** searches, website clicks and calls in Performance.
- **Map Pack:** search "marketing agency Seattle", "HVAC marketing Seattle" and "plumbing marketing Tacoma" from those areas (or use a free tool such as BrightLocal's rank checker).
- **Leads:** contact form and calls mentioning they found you on Google.
